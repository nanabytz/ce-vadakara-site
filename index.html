Welcome to CEV

Shaping Future Engineers & Innovators

College of Engineering Vadakara (CEV), formerly known as Co-operative Institute of Technology Vadakara, is the first engineering college established under the Co-operative Academy of Professional Education (CAPE) by the Government of Kerala in 1999.

Situated on the picturesque "Kakoramala" hilltop in Maniyur village, 13 km from Vadakara town, our 27-acre campus provides an idyllic environment for technical learning, research, and holistic development.

Principal's Message

Dear Students, Staffs and Parents,

Greetings from the College of Engineering Vadakara!

I hope this message finds you all in good health and high spirits. As we embark on a new academic year, I would like to extend a warm welcome to all the students of College of Engineering Vadakara.

It is my pleasure to welcome you all to the new academic year. Our college has a long and proud history of providing students with a comprehensive education, and we are dedicated to ensuring that every student has the tools and resources they need to succeed.

This year, we are focusing on making the college experience even more engaging, innovative, and student-centered. We have a talented and dedicated faculty and staff who are committed to your success, and we are always here to support you in any way we can.

In addition to a strong academic program, we also offer a wide range of extracurricular activities, clubs, and organizations that are designed to help you build your skills, make new friends, and have fun. I encourage you to take advantage of these opportunities and make the most of your college experience.
